About RGMTSI

Your Trusted Industrial Solutions Partner Since 2009

RG Manufacturing and Trading Solutions Inc. began its operations in October 2009. The company specializes in supplying industrial machinery, complete production lines, locally fabricated equipment, and integrated engineering solutions.

RGMTSI also markets industrial supplies such as vacuum belts, hot ink rolls, hot stamp ribbons and coder types, and its sales team and offices have expanded to meet market demand.

With a commitment to customer satisfaction, teamwork, and long-term partnerships, RGMTSI continuously works to provide dependable products and technical solutions to various industries.
